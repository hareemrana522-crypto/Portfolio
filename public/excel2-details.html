Corporate Financial Reporting Tool

Automated macro script that consolidates 50+ Excel sheets into one unified financial report in seconds.

The Challenge

Generating monthly financial reports required manually opening, copying, and pasting data from over 50 department-specific Excel sheets. This process took several hours, was prone to copy-paste errors, and delayed critical business decisions.

The Solution

A robust Excel VBA system using Power Query to automate the entire data consolidation pipeline:

  • One-Click Consolidation: A master macro that loops through a designated folder, extracts required financial metrics, and appends them to a master database.
  • Data Cleansing: Automated formatting and error-checking during data ingestion to ensure 100% data integrity.
  • Dynamic Dashboarding: The consolidated data feeds directly into pivot tables and interactive charts, providing instant executive insights.

Key Results

  • Time Saved: Reduced report generation time from 8 hours to under 30 seconds.
  • Accuracy: Eliminated 100% of human copy-paste errors.
  • Ease of Use: Provided a simple "Update Report" button, allowing non-technical staff to generate complex reports effortlessly.
