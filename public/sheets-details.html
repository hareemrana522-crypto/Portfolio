HR Operations & Performance Management System

Automated Google Sheets Architecture with Google Apps Script Daily Email Alerts

The Challenge

Manual employee tracking across scattered files led to unmonitored contract renewals, calculation errors in variable commissions, and zero centralized visibility over operational risks.

The Solution

An interconnected, relational Google Sheets system backed by Google Apps Script that automates workforce tracking and payroll:

  • Executive KPI Dashboard: Real-time visibility into active headcount, overdue agreements, and total monthly payroll liabilities.
  • Master Staff Directory: Automatically computes renewal deadlines and flags immediate compliance risks (FLAG: Overdue, Expiring Soon).
  • Automated Compensation Engine: Consolidates base salaries, attendance incentives, and sales commission tiers into final payouts.
  • Background Email Automation: Daily serverless Apps Script trigger scanning records at 9:00 AM to email HR action alerts directly.

Key Results

  • 100% Automated Calculations: Eliminated manual errors across salaries, bonuses, and commission slabs.
  • Zero Compliance Lapses: Automated email alerts ensure no contract or agreement deadline is missed.
  • Infinite Scalability: Open-ended formulas (A2:A) adapt dynamically from 5 to 1,000+ records.
