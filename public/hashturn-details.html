Project Overview

HashTurn designs and builds custom automation systems — connecting apps, eliminating repetitive tasks, and giving teams back hundreds of hours every month.

The goal of this project was to establish a digital presence for HashTurn that immediately communicates value: eliminating robot work so teams can focus on high-value human work. The platform highlights their core competencies in Business Process Automation, Robotic Process Automation (RPA), API integrations, and Microsoft 365 solutions.

The Problem It Solves

Modern teams lose countless hours daily to data entry, copy-pasting between apps, sending repetitive emails, and generating manual reports. HashTurn's automation solutions intercept these broken workflows and replace them with seamless, error-free background processes.
